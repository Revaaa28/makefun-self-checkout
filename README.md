Entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/u-motasimfuad-the-self-checkout-is-judging-you-b71130d6) bounty **The Self-Checkout Is Judging You** — one funny line in the voice of a smug self-checkout machine, 200 characters or less, to a shopper buying twelve yoghurts and nothing else.

## The line

> Please wait. I am verifying that twelve yogurts counts as a meal. It does not. It counts as a personality trait.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, stdlib only, no network, no randomness) deterministically writes the line above to `/output/entry.txt` — exactly 112 characters, one line, no trailing newline.

## Why this line

It follows the winning one-liner shape: a machine procedure in the till's own voice ("Please wait. I am verifying..."), a scene that escalates (verify → deny → reclassify), and a twist at the very end — the basket is not a meal, it is a personality trait.
